### Problem Statement:

You are given an `m x n` integer grid `accounts` where `accounts[i][j]` is the amount of money the `i​​​​​^​​​​​​th​​​​` customer has in the `j​​​​​^​​​​​​th`​​​​ bank. Return_ the **wealth** that the richest customer has._

A customer's **wealth** is the amount of money they have in all their bank accounts. The richest customer is the customer that has the maximum **wealth**.

> [!example]- Example 1
> **Input:**
>
> ```python
> accounts = [[1,2,3],[3,2,1]]
> ```
>
> **Output:**
>
> ```python
> 6
> ```
>
> **Explanation:**
>
> ```python
> **:**
> `1st customer has wealth = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6
> ``2nd customer has wealth = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6
> `Both customers are considered the richest with a wealth of 6 each, so return 6.
> ```

> [!example]- Example 2
> **Input:**
>
> ```python
> accounts = [[1,5],[7,3],[3,5]]
> ```
>
> **Output:**
>
> ```python
> 10
> ```
>
> **Explanation:**
>
> ```python
> :
> 1st customer has wealth = 6
> 2nd customer has wealth = 10
> 3rd customer has wealth = 8
> The 2nd customer is the richest with a wealth of 10.
> ```

> [!example]- Example 3
> **Input:**
>
> ```python
> accounts = [[2,8,7],[7,1,3],[1,9,5]]
> ```
>
> **Output:**
>
> ```python
> 17
> ```

> [!warning]- Constraints
> - `m == accounts.length`
>
> 	- `n == accounts[i].length`
>
> 	- `1 <= m, n <= 50`
>
> 	- `1 <= accounts[i][j] <= 100`

>
> ```python
> class Solution:
>     def maximumWealth(self, accounts: List[List[int]]) -> int:
>         maxWealth = 0
>         for acc in accounts:
>             maxWealth = max(maxWealth, sum(acc))
>
>         return maxWealth
> ```
